Principles

  1. Built, not imagined: real artefacts instead of robots, brains and glow.
  2. Stand out from the feed: colours that differ from LinkedIn's own in light and dark mode.
  3. One idea, one glance: one point per slide, made visible with a mini chart where it helps.
  4. Face first: the author is visible on every piece.
  5. Excited, not hyped: energy from colour, scale and contrast, never hype words or emoji.

Colour

Pop pastels in two roles: pastel backgrounds for covers and section slides; warm paper with pastel accents for content slides. Coral is the signature.

Rules

  • Covers use a pastel background. Only the cover shows in the feed, and LinkedIn's own feed is warm off-white (#F4F2EE) or neutral dark (#1B1F23), so a pastel cover stands out in both.
  • Pastels are fills, never text. They're too light to read on paper; use ink on them, and deep coral when a word needs colour.
  • One ink-outlined card per slide. The outline marks the single idea that matters.
  • Charts: one coral value, everything else paper grey. The chart should make one point at a glance.
  • Good versus bad without red and green. "Before" sits in a muted hairline card; "after" in an ink-outlined card with butter on the winning phrase.
  • No dark slides on LinkedIn. Revisit when a website or talks need them.
