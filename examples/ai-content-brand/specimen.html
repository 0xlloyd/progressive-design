Principles

  1. Built, not imagined: real artefacts instead of robots, brains and glow.
  2. Stand out from the feed: colours that differ from LinkedIn's own in light and dark mode.
  3. One idea, one glance: one point per slide, made visible with a mini chart where it helps.
  4. Face first: the author is visible on every piece.
  5. Excited, not hyped: energy from colour, scale and contrast, never hype words or emoji.

Rules

  • Covers use a pastel background, coral most often. Only the cover shows in the feed, and LinkedIn's feed is warm off-white or neutral dark, so pastels stand out in both.
  • Pastels are fills, never text. Ink on pastel; deep coral when a word needs colour.
  • One ink-outlined card per slide. The outline marks the single idea that matters.
  • Good versus bad without red and green. Muted hairline card for "before"; ink outline and butter highlight for "after".
  • No dark slides on LinkedIn. Revisit for a website or talks.

Typography

Onest (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use) at two weights, 400 and 900. No monospace.

Rules

  • Nothing below 32px on a slide. Phones show slides at about a third of their size.
  • 900 for headlines, stats and names; 400 for everything else.
  • Headlines 1.05 line height, −1.5% tracking; body 1.45, no tracking.
  • Sentence case, no full stops on headlines, body never past three lines.

Space, layout and shape

Space and layout

  • Airy density, 8px base: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128.
  • Slide grid: 96px margins, 6 columns, 32px gutters.
  • Left-aligned and top-anchored, never centred.
  • Covers: headshot top right, headline below, name bottom left.
  • Content footer: headshot, name, progress "2 / 7".

Shape and elevation

  • Radius: 0 slides, 12 small, 24 cards and photos, pill for tags and chips.
  • Borders: 2px hairline #E6DFCF; 4px ink for the key card.
  • No shadows: depth from surface colour and outlines.
  • Texture: graph-paper grid only, never on covers.

Infographic kit

One chart per slide, labelled directly, with coral marking the point the headline makes. No pie charts, 3D or gradients.

Imagery, motion and voice

Imagery

  • The same real headshot everywhere, cut into a circle with a coral, butter or lilac tile offset behind it.
  • Only real artefacts from your own work, cropped tight and placed in cards.
  • No stock photos, AI-generated people, robots, brains or sparkles.
  • Icons: Phosphor Bold, in ink, 64px or larger, only where they carry meaning.

Motion

  • None on LinkedIn. For web and video later: 120ms and 240ms, one easing curve, motion only for state changes, reduced motion respected.

Voice

  • Your own voice: warm, specific, explained through a real example.
  • Hooks lead with a specific outcome and a number.
  • Step headlines are imperatives with a concrete object.
  • The end slide makes one specific ask.
  • Avoid: game-changer, unlock, supercharge, seamless, revolutionise, delve, emoji bullets, all caps, em dashes.
